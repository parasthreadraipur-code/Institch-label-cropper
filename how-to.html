How to Use InStitch

Process Amazon, Flipkart, and Meesho shipping labels in under a minute

Getting started

1
Download your label PDFs from Amazon Seller Central, Flipkart Seller Hub, or the Meesho Supplier Panel — the same PDFs you'd normally send straight to your printer.
2
Open InStitch and drag your PDF file onto the upload area, or tap "Choose PDF file". You can upload files from any of the three platforms — InStitch detects which one automatically, so there's no need to select a platform first.
3
Wait a few seconds while InStitch reads the PDF, strips out anything you don't need (like Amazon's invoice pages), and extracts each label's SKU and quantity.
4
Review the labels in the results grid. Untick any you don't want to include, or use the search box to find a specific order.
5
Download your selected labels (or all of them) as a single print-ready PDF, sorted and cleaned up for packing.

Adding SKU translations

If you want each label to show a translated or simplified product name below the SKU — useful when your packing staff read a different language than English — InStitch can add that automatically.

1
Click ⬇ Template at the top of the page to download a starter CSV file with the correct column format.
2
Open the CSV in Excel or Google Sheets. Fill in your SKUs in the first column and the matching translation or note in the second column — this can be in any language, not just Hindi.
3
Save the file, then click 📋 Load SKU sheet and select it. InStitch remembers this sheet in your browser, so you only need to upload it once — it'll still be there next time you open the app on the same device.
💡 Tip: the translation appears as a small strip directly below each label, alongside the quantity — the original label content (barcode, address, SKU table) is never modified or covered.

Frequently needed actions

  • Wrong SKU sheet loaded? Just load a new CSV — it merges with (and overwrites matching entries in) whatever was already loaded.
  • Want to start over? Use the "Start over" button in the results view to clear everything and upload a fresh file.
  • Using InStitch offline? Once you've opened it while online at least once, it keeps working without an internet connection — your browser caches the app itself (not your label files).
