About InStitch

A free shipping-label processor for Amazon, Flipkart & Meesho sellers

🔒 Runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded

What is InStitch?

InStitch is a free tool for e-commerce sellers to process shipping labels from Amazon, Flipkart, and Meesho — all in one place, right in your browser.

It was built by a seller who was tired of switching between three different label formats every single day. InStitch auto-detects the platform, strips unnecessary pages, and reorganizes labels so packing is faster and less error-prone.

Privacy Policy

InStitch is designed with a simple privacy principle: your files never leave your device.

  • All PDF processing (reading, cropping, reorganizing) happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded to any server.
  • Any SKU-to-language sheet you upload is saved only in your own browser's local storage, so it's available next time you open the app on that device. It is never transmitted anywhere.
  • We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or account logins, and we do not collect, store, or have access to any shipping labels, customer names, addresses, or order details you process.
  • InStitch is a Progressive Web App and can work offline after your first visit, since the app files are cached locally by your browser.
  • If InStitch integrates advertising (e.g. Google AdSense) in the future, third-party ad providers may use cookies or similar technology to serve ads. We will update this policy if and when that changes, and any such advertising has no access to the labels or files you process.
